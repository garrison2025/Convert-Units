# Google SEO numerical-results launch policy — V3

Version date: 2026-11-02. Supersedes the earlier **26-value pilot** in `SEO-LAUNCH-PILOT-20261102.md`. The earlier document is retained as a historical audit of how this decision evolved, **not** the active release plan.

## Decision and scope

**Active default:** 38 dedicated directional converters + 16 categories + 10 other site routes + **15** value-specific numeric answer pages are eligible for a verified official-domain sitemap: **79 canonical URLs**.

All 92 numeric answer pages continue to render and work. Of these, **77 are intentionally not selected for indexing**: they have `noindex,follow` in a confirmed production-domain build and do not have a self-canonical or sitemap entry. These remain accessible via their direct URLs. The temporary `*.pages.dev` deployment is globally `noindex` regardless of the policy.

**Rationale:** 26 initial numeric pages covered 15 actual direction topics, yielding **17/17 same-direction pairs** above the audit's normalized-template similarity threshold. Under V3, there is at most **one** potentially indexable numeric answer for each conversion direction. This removes same-direction numeric/numeric pairs from the initial *index candidate set*; **it does not prove the remaining 15 are unique against their parent tools or will rank on Google**. The parent calculator remains the main utility.

Google Search Central explains that duplicate content can be normal and Google chooses canonical URLs by its own assessment; avoiding index proliferation is a release-risk decision, not evidence of a penalty.

## Release controls

- Source of truth: `src/data/numeric-seo-policy.json` version 2. All SEO generation and build checks use this one file.
- `NUMERIC_INDEX_MODE=curated` is the default. `NUMERIC_INDEX_MODE=all` requires the separate `NUMERIC_INDEX_CONFIRMATION=REVIEWED_ALL_92` override; **do not set it casually**.
- CI rejects any curated list with two eligible numeric answer pages belonging to one conversion direction, and checks all 92 values still work.
- Each CI run emits `dist/keyword-evidence-review.csv` with the explicit 15/77 technical policy status, while editorial status remains unverified.
- `dist/seo-url-inventory.csv`, `seo-content-audit.json`, `seo-crawl-audit.json` and `release-audit-report.json` validate static links, canonical targets, sitemap inclusion and content availability.
- Do not add arbitrary FAQ paragraphs, synthetic author biographies, unsupported pageview forecasts, duplicate local-language variations, or build-date-as-`lastmod` markup to disguise repetitive content.

## Outstanding evidence gates

Before acquiring a domain or expanding the index, examine Google US desktop/mobile top-ten results for the parent tool page and the representative value query in each high-priority direction. Record search features, intent, competing content and whether the query merits its own page. Then launch only when desired and use **actual GSC page/query impressions and clicks**, canonical selections, and real user workflow data to compare the 15 value pages to the parent tools. **Do not treat a passed static audit as Google approval.**
